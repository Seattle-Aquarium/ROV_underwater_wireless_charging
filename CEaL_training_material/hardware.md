\### ROV Hardware



The Seattle Aquarium's third BlueROV2 -- alongside ROV *Lutris* and ROV *Nereo* -- is comprised of the following:



* Raspberry Pi ([Pi](https://bluerobotics.com/store/comm-control-power/control/raspberry-pi/)) computer and Navigator Flight Controller ([Navigator](https://bluerobotics.com/store/comm-control-power/control/navigator/)) within an acrylic 4" diameter [electronics enclosure](https://bluerobotics.com/store/rov/bluerov2-components-spares/brov2-asm-electronics-r1/), rated to 100m depth.
* [150m tether](https://bluerobotics.com/store/cables-connectors/cables/fathom-rov-tether-rov-ready/) with quick connect/disconnect, [tether spool](https://bluerobotics.com/store/cables-connectors/tether-management/tms-asm-kit-vp/).
* Eight thrusters via the [Heavy](https://bluerobotics.com/store/rov/bluerov2-accessories/brov2-heavy-kit/) configuration.
* The lower [Payload Skid](https://bluerobotics.com/store/rov/bluerov2-accessories/brov-payload-skid/) which contains the Underwater Wireless Charging System ([UWSC](https://bluerobotics.com/store/comm-control-power/powersupplies-batteries/underwater-wireless-charging-system/)) control box and wireless charging prong.
* The 3" diameter [battery enclosure](https://bluerobotics.com/product-category/watertight-enclosures/locking-series/) is slightly longer than standard to enable proper battery cell balancing upon recharging via the UWSC.
* It has a daisy-chain of four [Lumen lights](https://bluerobotics.com/store/thrusters/lights/lumen-sets-r2-rp/), all four are controlled simultaneously via the Xbox controller -- two lights face forward, two lights face downward.
* It has two camera views you will see side-by-side in Cockpit: the primary *forward-facing* [Low-Light camera](https://bluerobotics.com/store/sensors-cameras/cameras/cam-usb-low-light-r1/) that can pivot up and down within the main electronics enclosure [dome port](https://bluerobotics.com/store/watertight-enclosures/locking-series/wte-end-cap-vp/), and a DeepWater Exploration (DWE) [exploreHD](https://bluerobotics.com/store/sensors-cameras/cameras/deepwater-exploration-explorehd-usb-camera/) static *downward-facing camera* mounted on the Payload Skid. 
* A compass in the Navigator provides ROV *heading* in Cockpit (though compass efficacy varies near the metal pier pilings).
* Inertial Measurement Units (IMU) in the Navigator provide *pitch*, *roll* in Cockpit.
* the [barometer](https://bluerobotics.com/store/sensors-cameras/sensors/bar-depth-pressure-sensor/) provides *depth* in Cockpit.
